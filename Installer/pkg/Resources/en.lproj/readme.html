Important Information

Please take a moment to read this before installing.

Updating

An older version of the driver is replaced completely. Printers you already set up with LabelWerk are kept and switched to the new PPD files automatically – your chosen settings (darkness, speed, label stock …) are preserved.

After installation

  1. Open @APP_NAME@ from the Applications folder. The setup assistant looks for connected and network printers (USB, Bonjour, network scan, Bluetooth).
  2. Select a printer and add it as a macOS printer with a single click – model, resolution and driver are matched automatically.
  3. Alternatively: System Settings › Printers & Scanners › Add Printer and choose “Zebra … – LabelWerk” as the driver.

Privacy

@APP_NAME@ only talks to your printers on the local network, via USB or Bluetooth. No data is sent to third parties. macOS asks for permission the first time the app accesses the local network or Bluetooth.

Uninstalling

Use the Driver & Queues section in the app or run sudo /Library/Printers/LabelWerk/uninstall.sh in Terminal (options: --help).
